Investment Options

Systematic Investment Plan (SIP)

SIP lets you invest a fixed amount regularly in mutual funds. It benefits from rupee cost averaging — you buy more units when prices are low.

  • Suitable for long-term wealth creation
  • Market-linked returns (not guaranteed)
  • High liquidity in most equity funds
  • Minimum ₹500/month typically

Past performance does not guarantee future returns.

Fixed & Recurring Deposits

Bank FDs offer guaranteed returns at a fixed interest rate. RDs allow monthly deposits like SIP but at fixed bank rates.

  • Capital protection (up to DICGC limits)
  • Fixed, predictable returns
  • Premature withdrawal penalties
  • Returns are taxable as per income slab

Public Provident Fund (PPF)

Government-backed long-term savings scheme with 15-year lock-in. One of the best tax-saving instruments in India.

  • Tax-free returns (EEE status)
  • Government-backed, very safe
  • 15-year lock-in (partial withdrawal allowed after 7 years)
  • Max ₹1.5L per year investment

Gold Investment

Gold acts as an inflation hedge and safe haven. Options include physical gold, Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB), and Gold ETFs.

  • SGBs earn 2.5% annual interest
  • Gold ETFs traded like stocks
  • Physical gold has storage risks
  • Long-term hedge against inflation
